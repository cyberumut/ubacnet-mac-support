U-BACnet for Mac — Support

Discover, read, write and monitor BACnet/IP building automation devices from your Mac.

Questions, bug reports or feature requests? Reach us through the contact page — we usually answer within two business days.

Getting started

  1. Connect your Mac to the same network (or VLAN) as your BACnet/IP controllers.
  2. Open U-BACnet and click Start Scanning. Devices answer the Who-Is broadcast and appear in the sidebar.
  3. Select a device to load its points, then select a point to see its value, properties and live chart.
  4. No devices at hand? Choose Explore the Demo Network (or File › Explore Demo Network) to try every feature against a simulated building.

Troubleshooting

  • No devices found — make sure macOS allowed U-BACnet to use the local network (System Settings › Privacy & Security › Local Network), that UDP port 47808 (0xBAC0) is not blocked, and that you picked the right interface in Settings › Network.
  • Devices on another subnet — BACnet broadcasts don't cross routers; use a BBMD on the remote network or connect to that subnet.
  • Writes are rejected — the device may require a different priority, or the point may be read-only or out of service.
  • Acknowledging an alarm fails — some controllers require a specific acknowledging source or a password; check the controller's alarm configuration.

Supported services

Who-Is/I-Am, Who-Has/I-Have, ReadProperty, ReadPropertyMultiple, WriteProperty, ReadRange (Trend Log), SubscribeCOV and COV notifications, event notifications, GetAlarmSummary, AcknowledgeAlarm, TimeSynchronization, DeviceCommunicationControl and ReinitializeDevice.
