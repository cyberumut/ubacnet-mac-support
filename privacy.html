U-BACnet for Mac

Privacy Policy

Last updated 19 November 2026

U-BACnet for Mac does not collect, transmit or store any personal data. There is no account, no sign-up, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party SDK in the app.

What the app does on your network

The app talks only to BACnet/IP devices on your local network, using the BACnet protocol over UDP. Everything it reads from those devices (device names, point values, alarms) stays on your Mac and is never sent to us or to anyone else.

Files you export

When you export data (CSV, JSON or EDE), the file is written only to the location you choose.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, alarm alerts are generated locally on your Mac.

Data collection

We do not collect usage data, diagnostics, identifiers, location or contact information. Apple may provide anonymous crash reports to developers only if you opt in to sharing them with app developers.

Children

The app is a professional tool and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions about this policy: ubacnet.com/contact.
